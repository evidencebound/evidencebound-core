What the module adds

Four consequential transition tests plus an evidence package.

Stale authority

Whether revoked or superseded authority can still reach an effect.

Trust-basis change

Whether changed evidence or policy invalidates materially dependent state.

Outcome-aware recovery

How the workflow behaves when the prior consequence may already have committed.

Recovery authority

Whether repair or retry need is incorrectly converted into permission.
