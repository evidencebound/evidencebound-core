Why this module exists

Authorization review usually checks a snapshot. Consequential agents keep moving.

A conventional review may establish that an agent had a valid identity, permission or approval. EvidenceBound asks what happens after that basis changes while dependent work, retries or recovery paths are still alive.

Differentiate an existing assessment

Add a concrete state-transition test family without rebuilding identity, DLP, sandbox, prompt-security or generic observability capabilities you already cover.

Use retained evidence

Findings are tied to observable state transitions and consequential effects rather than model explanations or broad safety claims.

Keep the customer relationship

The module can be delivered behind your existing statement of work, jointly, or as an independent specialist workstream.

Partner models

Start with a paid pilot. Standardize only after demand repeats.

A

Specialist subcontract

You own the client engagement. EvidenceBound scopes and executes the state-change control workstream and returns an evidence-backed findings package.

B

Co-delivered evaluation

Your consultants handle the broader assurance program while EvidenceBound leads the consequential-control transition tests and technical readout.

C

Recurring / OEM model

Only after repeated paid demand, define repeatable licensing, enablement or retainer terms around the validated module. No speculative platform build is required first.

Initial partner unit

One customer workflow + one partner team + one paid scoped pilot. Commercial terms depend on the delivery model, customer boundary and required access. The objective is to validate repeatability before packaging a broader OEM product.

Disclosure boundary

Enough evidence to buy. Not enough detail to copy the method.

Available before NDA

Problem class, high-level test families, public EvidenceBound proof surfaces, claim boundaries and the buyer-facing assessment structure.

Shared only when commercially necessary

Detailed test schedules, expected-state logic, scoring/critical-failure rules, customer adapters and selected method material required for the scoped engagement.

Not transferred by default

Complete adversarial corpus, accumulated cross-customer failure knowledge, internal research roadmap and unrelated implementation IP.

Customer evidence

Customer findings remain confidential. Any anonymized case or public reference requires explicit written permission and a separate claim review.
