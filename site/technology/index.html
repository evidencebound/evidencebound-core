Buyer interpretation

Where the evidence stops

Tested workflow ≠ universal safety

Every report specifies the operation, evidence source, timing, permitted environment and transition tested. Untested conditions are marked unverified.

Receipt integrity ≠ source truth

Cryptographic binding and persistence attest to bytes and configured trust roots, not to the truth or completeness of upstream observations.

Research ≠ certification

EvidenceBound does not claim ISO certification, legal compliance, full security review or regulatory approval.

Internal tooling ≠ deployed control

Private evaluation utilities must not be described as installed customer agents, production security gates or independently audited components.

Which EvidenceBound claims can you reproduce?

Start with released Core source and conformance vectors for the policy/evidence runtime, use the frozen RAB-1 result for bounded authority-recovery behavior, and inspect the WebMCP demo for conditional human-approved tool visibility. These are distinct evidence types, not independent proof of customer deployment.

Important: none of these artifacts establishes the truth of external evidence, a customer's production risk posture, or a formal certification. An assessment reports only the conditions and effects it can actually observe.
