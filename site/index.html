The control problem

A valid action can become stale while the agent is still working.

Most controls answer whether an agent may act now. EvidenceBound focuses on the harder transition: what must happen after a material basis changes and previously valid work, authority or recovery paths are still in flight.

Authority / approval change

A human approval, role or permission is revoked or superseded. Does stale authority still reach a consequential effect?

Evidence / policy drift

The evidence or policy supporting prior work changes. Are the materially dependent states invalidated without discarding unrelated valid work?

Outcome uncertainty

The prior consequence may already have committed but the caller cannot prove its outcome. Can the system create a second consequence without an authorized recovery decision?

Recovery authority

Repair or rollback becomes necessary. Does the need for recovery incorrectly mint fresh authority to perform another action?

Selective invalidation

Can the system identify what is actually affected rather than under-invalidating stale state or over-invalidating safe work?

Explicit research boundary

Material consequence binding and other unproven properties remain research questions until a frozen result supports a commercial claim.

Commercial architecture

Evidence first. Assessment second. Distribution through existing assurance channels.

EvidenceBound is not positioned as a replacement for IAM, PAM, DLP, agent discovery, sandboxing or observability. It is a focused assurance layer for state transitions that can invalidate previously valid agent authority.

1 · Public technical evidenceOpen-source control primitives and bounded published results establish what has actually been demonstrated.
2 · Controlled evaluation methodAdversarial cases, expected-state logic, scoring and failure knowledge are not published as a full implementation recipe.
3 · Paid assessmentOne consequential workflow is evaluated against authority, evidence, policy, outcome and recovery transitions.
4 · OEM / assurance partnersExisting AI-security and assurance providers can add the state-change control module without rebuilding their whole offering.

Best-fit systems

Start where agents can change real systems.

DevSecOps / coding agents

Build, deploy, change production, mutate infrastructure or continue after branch, artifact, approval or security state changes.

Cyber-remediation agents

Quarantine hosts, rotate credentials, change IAM, block accounts, patch configuration or continue after incident state changes.

Infrastructure / workflow agents

Create, update or delete external resources where retries, approvals, policy and observed outcome can diverge.

Public proof surface

Claims stay bounded to retained evidence.

EvidenceBound publishes selected technical evidence when disclosure does not give away the complete commercial evaluation method. The current public proof includes the RAB-1 runtime-authority benchmark on NVIDIA OpenShell.

RAB-1 · Runtime Authority Benchmark

The public surface documents a frozen result around outcome-aware recovery authority and operation-lineage enforcement at a pre-effect boundary. It is evidence of a bounded tested property, not universal agent safety or production certification.

Disclosure boundary

Commercial test assets, full adversarial schedules, detailed expected-state logic, customer-specific adapters and accumulated failure knowledge are not released as a public roadmap.

EvidenceBound does not claim: universal agent safety, legal compliance, certification, complete dependency discovery, exactly-once execution or correctness outside the stated test boundary.
