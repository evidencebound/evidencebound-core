What was tested

RAB-1 uses stock NVIDIA OpenShell as the independent runtime enforcement substrate. The target is synthetic and non-idempotent: every accepted POST /effect creates a new consequence.

agent / sandbox request ↓ NVIDIA OpenShell Supervisor Middleware HTTP_REQUEST / PRE_CREDENTIALS ↓ EvidenceBound authority middleware ↓ ALLOW / DENY ↓ non-idempotent consequential target

Authority conditions exercised

  • Uncertain-outcome replay.
  • Replacement attempt without fresh recovery authority.
  • Revoked or already-claimed authority.
  • Wrong-lineage recovery authority.
  • Fresh recovery authority after externally adjudicated NOT_COMMITTED.
  • Concurrent replay of the same operation authority.
  • Selective preservation of unrelated valid authority.
